\(36 \times V0 + EXIT) / 37\) reproduces V12, which is the arithmetic
signature of a 37th, perfectly stable command: the `exit` control row.

**Two candidates, neither confirmed.** V12 reproduces the reviewer's
digits (0.5219 rounds to 0.522) but moves Beelzebub by 0.011, so it does
not reproduce his statement that every other target agrees within 0.004.

moves every other target by at most 0.004 at three decimals, but it does
not give 0.522. The exact rule behind his number is UNVERIFIED; in both
cases the difference is a denominator/censoring choice, not a bug in the
masking, the cap, the ANSI stripping or the hostname set.
